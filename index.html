Up to three places to begin

My Tasks

Keep only what you want to remember. Small steps are welcome, and so is leaving things for later.

Brain Dump

A place to unload thoughts and responsibilities. Nothing here is a promise unless you choose to make it a task.

Ctrl+Enter or Cmd+Enter keeps it too.

Feeling Stuck

One small, achievable action at a time. You can ask for a different one, or stop. Both are fine.

If you already know the task, break it into a smaller step in My Tasks. The smallest version is a complete place to start.

Focus Room

Only one task, and a little time. When the time ends, you are allowed to stop.

My Garden

Plants grow when you complete tasks and small steps. They never die, and quiet days never take growth away.

    How the garden grows
    • A small step inside a task adds a little growth.
    • Completing a whole task adds a little more.
    • A focus session that runs until the end adds growth.
    • A tiny action from Feeling Stuck adds growth.
    • Noting rest, once in a day, adds growth too.

    Inactivity does nothing. Growth stays, even if you uncheck a task or take a long break.
